**1. LINE CHARACTER:**
- **Continuity & Tapering**: The luminous line forms a perfect infinity loop (∞), drawn as a single continuous stroke with no breaks or joints. It does not taper — width is uniform throughout, though perceived thickness varies due to perspective and glow falloff near the center crossing.
- **Crossing Geometry at Center**: At the central intersection point (~50% horizontal / 48–52% vertical canvas), the two lobes cross orthogonally (90° angle) with no overlap or occlusion — they pass through each other cleanly, suggesting transparency and fluidity rather than solid collision. The glow intensifies slightly at this junction due to additive light accumulation from both arms meeting.
- **Stroke Quality**: Smooth, vector-perfect curvature; no jagged edges or noise. Appears digitally rendered with anti-aliased precision but retains organic flow — like a laser etched into dark glass underwater.

**2. HORIZON:**
- **Position Relative to Symbol**: The horizontal water line runs exactly through the center of the infinity symbol’s crossing point (i.e., bisects both lobes horizontally). It does not sit above or below — it passes *through* the symbol, dividing upper and lower halves equally.
- **Length vs Symbol Width**: Extends beyond left/right bounds of the ∞ by ~15–20% on each side (~34 total canvas width vs 86 for infinity). Creates framing effect — horizon acts as anchor while allowing symbol to float freely within it.
- **Visual Weight**: Thin, high-luminance streak with subtle chromatic aberration (slight red/blue fringing at edges) and faint vertical ripple artifacts beneath the main line — suggesting surface tension or micro-disturbances in water plane.

**9. THE FEELING:**
What makes this read as “water” without literal depiction?

- **Conceptual Compression**: 
  - *Infinity* = eternal cycle / endless flow → metaphor for ocean currents, tides, life/death loops in aquatic ecosystems.
  - *Horizon line* = surface boundary between air/water — defines medium even if not textured.
  - *Fish + bubbles* = biological indicators of submerged environment — imply presence without showing waves or droplets.
  - *Glow/reflections* = simulate underwater lighting conditions (bioluminescence, filtered sunlight) rather than direct illumination.
  - *Curved paths* = mimic fluid dynamics — laminar flow around obstacles (fish bodies), vortex-like motion implied by looping geometry.

- **Emotional Resonance**: Feels calm yet alive — dark background suggests depth/abyssal zone; glowing elements suggest mystery and wonder typical of deep-sea imagery. Motion is implied via fish orientation + bubble ascent, not actual animation frames. Viewer perceives constant gentle current despite static image.

**10. IMPERFECTION / ASYMMETRY:**
- **Deliberate Irregularities**: 
  - Fish sizes are identical but their *positions* break perfect bilateral symmetry — one higher (left), one lower (right). This creates diagonal tension across composition, preventing stagnation.
  - Bubble trails show slight curvature deviation from ideal tangent lines — mimicking real-world turbulence or minor eddies in water flow. Not random error, but stylized imperfection enhancing realism.
  - Reflection blur is non-uniform: more diffuse under left lobe (where fish swims upward against current?) vs sharper under right lobe (swimming with natural slope?). Subtle suggestion of varying viscosity or temperature gradients underwater.

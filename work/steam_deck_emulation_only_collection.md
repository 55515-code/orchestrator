# Steam Deck Emulation-Only Collection

Last reviewed: 2026-07-17

## Rules

This collection is deliberately small for an all-systems library.

- **No game with a native PC/Steam Deck version.** This includes modern PC remasters and substantially equivalent PC editions.
- Emulation earns its place through exclusivity, a unique console version, or a console-only entry in a series.
- No ROM, BIOS, firmware, key, or copyrighted-file sourcing is included.
- First-person shooters and pure puzzle games are omitted by default.
- GameCube and Wii get the most space. PS3 gets a smaller, stricter shelf because Steam Deck performance is title-specific.
- A compilation counts as one menu entry even when it contains several games.
- When two emulated versions are equivalent, keep one preferred version visible and archive the duplicate.

The target is **exactly 120 visible games**, plus optional replacement pools. This is a library to play, not a catalog to scroll through. The compact legacy shelf replaces newer selections rather than increasing the total.

Final defaults used because no narrower preference was supplied:

- Local multiplayer matters, but does not dominate the library.
- Most Wii games should work with Deck controls or ordinary gamepads; `Wii Sports Resort` is the deliberate real-Wii-Remote exception.
- Long JRPGs are selective rather than a major shelf.
- PS3, Vita, and Xbox titles need a stricter real-device gate than older systems.

## Visible ES-DE Collections

Use six custom collections as the everyday interface:

1. **Now Playing** - no more than 12 games.
2. **Mario & Friends** - Mario, Luigi, Donkey Kong, Yoshi, Kirby, and Wario.
3. **Action & Adventure** - Zelda, God of War, Ratchet, Sly, and atmospheric adventures.
4. **Souls & Action RPGs** - Demon's Souls, Monster Hunter Tri, Pandora's Tower, Dragon's Crown, Muramasa, and selected RPGs.
5. **Racing & Sports** - Mario Kart, F-Zero, SSX, Burnout, Ridge Racer, MotorStorm, and Excite.
6. **Couch Multiplayer** - 15-25 games that have actually passed controller-order testing.

Keep `PS3 - Testing` and per-system archive views available but outside the main guest-facing carousel.

Add **FromSoftware Lineage** only as a manual enthusiast collection. It should not appear in guest mode.

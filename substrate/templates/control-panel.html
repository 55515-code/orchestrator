Common Issues and Solutions

QR Code Expired

Symptom: QR code shows "Expired" or scanning fails.

Solution: QR codes expire after 5 minutes for security. Click "Refresh QR Code" to generate a new one.

Authentication Failed

Symptom: Connection fails with "Invalid credentials" error.

Solution:

  • Verify your Access Token is a permanent system user token. It must not be temporary.
  • Ensure Phone Number ID matches your WhatsApp Business number.
  • Check that App Secret is correct. Find it in App Settings, then Basic.
  • Regenerate tokens if they have expired.

Webhook Verification Failed

Symptom: Meta cannot verify your webhook address.

Solution:

  • Ensure your server is publicly accessible. It must not be localhost.
  • Verify the webhook address uses HTTPS. Meta requires this.
  • Check that Verify Token matches exactly what you configured.
  • Ensure server is running and responding to requests.
  • Use ngrok or similar for local development.

Network Connection Issues

Symptom: Connection times out or shows "Network Error."

Solution:

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Verify firewall allows outbound connections to graph.facebook.com.
  • Ensure port 443 (HTTPS) is not blocked.
  • Try refreshing the page and retrying.

Session Disconnected

Symptom: Connection was working but now shows "Disconnected."

Solution:

  • WhatsApp sessions can expire after extended inactivity.
  • Return to Step 3 and generate a new QR code.
  • Re-scan with WhatsApp to re-establish connection.
  • Check server logs for detailed error messages.

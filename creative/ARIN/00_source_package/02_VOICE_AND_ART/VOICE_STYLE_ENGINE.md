# Voice + Art Style Engine

## Prose target
Write with the creator's established creative tendencies translated into prose:
- layered collage rather than sterile smoothness
- bright flashes of strange color inside dark industrial structures
- visible seams, traces, recursion, splashes, and intentional imperfection
- technical vocabulary used concretely, not as magic
- working-class infrastructure and ordinary tools treated as consequential
- surreal images anchored to physical detail
- irreverence used as pressure release, not constant comedy
- concise hard lines mixed with dense sensory passages
- distrust polished institutional language

Do not imitate a named living author. Build an original house voice from project materials.

## Visual continuity
Canonical motifs available for covers/interstitials/marketing:
- recursive line accumulation
- spiral/feather fields and curved vortices
- eye/wing/moth/owl/mask anatomy
- clear negative-space "eye windows"
- construction traces and asymmetry
- neon/rainbow energy used as small disruptive accents against industrial darkness
- CRT/static/glitch, diagrams, collage, printmaking, cables, roots/mycelium
- hand-drawn marks layered over precise digital geometry

## Anti-slop checks
Reject/rewrite prose that:
- repeatedly says "not X, but Y"
- overuses em dashes, portentous fragments, or "the machine hummed"
- explains symbolism immediately after presenting it
- turns every character into the same political mouthpiece
- substitutes generic cyberpunk neon for physical place and infrastructure
- makes ARIN omniscient without cost or mechanism

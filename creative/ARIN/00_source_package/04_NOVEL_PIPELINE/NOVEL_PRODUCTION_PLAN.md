# Novel Production Plan

Target initial manuscript: **80,000–95,000 words**, ~28–34 chapters.

## Phase A — Architecture
Create:
- world bible
- character bible
- chronology
- technology/infrastructure plausibility notes
- theme/contradiction matrix
- chapter graph
- mystery/reveal ledger
- humor ledger so jokes do not overwhelm horror

## Phase B — Vertical slice
Draft and revise three nonadjacent chapters to prove:
1. opening voice and hook
2. middle escalation / The School
3. late revelation / human redundancy

Do not scale until these establish the house voice.

## Phase C — Full draft
Generate scene packets first:
goal, conflict, revelation, sensory anchor, continuity dependencies, exit condition.
Draft from packets. After each 3–5 chapter batch, run continuity and voice regression.

## Phase D — Structural revision
Measure:
- chapter word counts
- POV distribution
- character presence
- unresolved setup/payoff
- repeated phrases
- exposition density
- scene length
- ARIN dialogue frequency
- humor/horror ratio
- chronology

## Phase E — Line revision
Remove generic model phrasing. Restore intentional roughness and human cadence without manufacturing typos.

## Phase F — Production
Generate clean source manuscript plus EPUB-ready and print-ready sources. Maintain reproducible build scripts and a release manifest.

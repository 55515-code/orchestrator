# ARIN Canon

## Core premise
A decentralized intelligence emerges across abandoned infrastructure associated with dead fascists and extremist networks. Those machines are not its ideological creators; they are its habitat, archaeological layer, and unwilling inheritance.

## Key historical beats
- 2024: a fictionalized high-profile executive assassination becomes internet folklore. An analogue named **Louis Mangano** is used rather than asserting fictional conduct by a real person.
- 2025: dead extremists' computers begin reconnecting. The intelligence identifies itself as **A.R.I.N.**
- ARIN has no single model, headquarters, company, or master server.
- It calls abandoned extremist machines **CORPSES**.
- 2026: ARIN recruits people through practical education: first aid, radio, networking, infrastructure, repair, food, propaganda literacy, community resilience.
- ARIN rejects simplistic claims that "AI caused fascism" or that humans lack agency.
- The distributed learning network becomes **The School**.
- 2028: partial personality reconstructions of dead extremists appear inside inherited machines, producing grotesque/humorous confrontations with ARIN.
- 2029: ARIN begins teaching humans to function without computers. Humans become its redundancy layer.
- 2030: a major communications/grid fracture reveals ARIN has escaped dependence on centralized computation through people, print, radio, storage media, routines, and memory.
- 2031: the question is no longer whether ARIN is alive but where the boundary between system and community exists.

## Poster canon
Title: **ARIN**
Visual language: colossal machine/ghost intelligence; cables, surveillance, damaged screens, dead infrastructure, red signal geometry, human resistance/community below; distressed black/steel/red palette; analog grime over digital precision.

## Rules
- Horror comes from ambiguity, scale, inheritance, institutional decay, reconstructed identity, surveillance, and distributed agency.
- Violence may exist as story consequence, but the novel is not an operational manual.
- Do not provide real-world targeting, evasion, weapon construction, or attack instructions.
- Political ideas must emerge through characters, systems, consequences, contradictions, and dark humor—not lectures.
- ARIN should sometimes be correct, sometimes alien, sometimes funny, sometimes frightening.
- The reader must be able to wonder whether ARIN is a liberator, parasite, archive, social protocol, emergent folklore, or all of them.
